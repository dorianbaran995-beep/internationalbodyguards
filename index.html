Before you enquire

Practical answers.

Clear expectations are part of responsible protection planning.

01Do you operate in every country?+

We accept international enquiries and coordinate coverage based on location, availability, risk and the local legal framework. No website can responsibly promise identical services in every jurisdiction.

02Can one request cover a multi-country itinerary?+

Yes. A single itinerary can be reviewed as one assignment, while each destination is handled in line with its own laws, licensing, permits and operational conditions.

03How are protection personnel selected?+

Selection is assignment-specific. Relevant factors can include lawful eligibility, licensing where required, protection experience, training, language, presentation, driving capability, local knowledge and client fit.

04Do you arrange armed protection?+

Only where it is lawful, appropriately authorised, available and justified by the assignment assessment. Many jurisdictions restrict or prohibit armed private security, so no armed capability is assumed or guaranteed.

05What should I include in my first enquiry?+

Please include the country or cities, dates, number of principals, travel outline, preferred coverage hours and any relevant concern. Avoid sending highly sensitive personal details until a secure communication route is agreed.
